## Project Overview

Hardware schematics, PCB design, and embedded C firmware for the **University of Brighton Robot Wars Competition** (modules XE521 Engineering Design & EO524 Embedded Systems).

Our autonomous combat robot achieved a **2nd Place Podium Finish** in the inter-collegiate arena tournament and earned an **82% Distinction Grade (A+)**.

---

## 🏆 Key Features

- **Podium Finish (2nd Place):** Survived intense bracket elimination rounds against competing collegiate robotics teams.
- **High-Current H-Bridge Motor Driver:** Custom dual-channel MOSFET driver schematic engineered and verified in **Labcenter Proteus Design Suite**.
- **Ultrasonic Sonar Guidance:** Real-time obstacle evasion and target engagement routines driven by hardware timer capture of HC-SR04 ultrasonic echo pulses.
- **Embedded C Control Firmware:** Modular state machine executing differential steering, weapon actuation, and diagnostic logging to an onboard LCD screen.
