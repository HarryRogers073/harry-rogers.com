## Project Overview

A digital signal processing and hardware filter design suite developed for the **EO626 - Digital Signal Processing** engineering module at the **University of Brighton**.

The project synthesizes digital filter algorithms from analog transfer function prototypes and verifies them through time-domain and frequency-domain acoustic simulation before exporting them into synthesizable HDL for FPGA hardware acceleration.

---

## 🔬 Core Capabilities

- **Bilinear Transformation with Frequency Pre-Warping:** Converts continuous $s$-domain Butterworth prototypes into discrete $z$-domain transfer functions.
- **FIR Window Synthesis:** Designs linear-phase FIR filters using windowed sinc truncation and Kaiser/Hamming tapering.
- **Simulink Acoustic Pipeline:** Dynamic multi-band audio filtering simulation comparing raw wave inputs, injected acoustic interference, and filtered outputs.
- **Automated HDL Coder Generation:** Generates synthesizable Verilog/VHDL RTL filter cores from MATLAB transfer functions.
